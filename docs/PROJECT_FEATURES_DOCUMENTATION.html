1. Executive Summary

The Coimbatore Smart City Digital Twin is a cyber-physical urban intelligence platform representing the key physical, infrastructural, and temporal dynamics of Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. The platform models urban flow across three core pillars:

  • Intelligent Mobility & Traffic Dynamics: Real-time arterial tracking, multi-horizon ML forecasting, TreeSHAP explainability, and downstream spillover propagation.
  • Energy Grid & Power Demand: Substation demand monitoring across 5 distribution zones with 24-hour predictive load curves.
  • Environmental Intelligence: Live atmospheric air quality index (AQI) tracking and localized traffic emission correlation modeling.

2. Key Feature Pillars

1. Interactive 2.5D/3D Digital Twin Map GIS Engine

Built with MapLibre GL JS and Deck.gl, featuring 2.5D perspective rendering (45° pitch, -15° bearing) centered over Coimbatore [76.9850, 11.0250].

  • Arterial Coverage: Avinashi Road (NH 544), Trichy Road (NH 81), Sathy Road (NH 948), Mettupalayam Road (NH 181), and Pollachi Road (NH 83).
  • Congestion Glow Shading: Corridors dynamically glow in Emerald Green (<30), Amber (30-60), Orange (60-85), or Red (≥85).
  • Click-to-Inspect: Interactively select any road segment to view real-time speeds, vehicle counts, and multi-horizon forecasts.
  • Pulsing Incident Markers: Visual alerts on map for road accidents and construction closures.

2. Traffic Intelligence & Multi-Horizon ML Forecasting LightGBM

Uses Greenshields density equilibrium scoring combined with LightGBM regressors to forecast congestion levels across 4 temporal horizons:

  • 15 Minutes: Intersection shockwave warning
  • 30 Minutes: Dynamic traffic signal timing adaptation
  • 60 Minutes: Regional route diversion recommendations
  • 120 Minutes: Municipal shift management and incident clearance

3. Explainable AI (XAI) Cause Attribution Engine TreeSHAP

Decomposes forward predictions into normalized percentage contributions across four distinct municipal root causes:

  • Peak-Hour Commuter Surge: Morning and evening rush-hour volume.
  • Upstream Junction Queue Spillover: Congestion shockwaves propagating from adjacent intersections.
  • Active Incident / Lane Capacity Loss: Flyover construction pier erection or multi-vehicle collisions.
  • Weather & Rain Conditions: Wet pavement and visibility throttling.

4. Downstream Spillover & Carbon Emission Engine EPA VSP Model

Evaluates network diversion when a corridor bottlenecks, calculating queue growth and environmental penalties:

  • Alternate Corridor Diversion: Calculates volume rerouted (e.g. from Avinashi Road to Trichy Road via Sungam Bypass).
  • Wasted Fuel Calculation: Estimates excess liters of fuel burned during vehicular idling.
  • Carbon Footprint Delta: Excess kg of CO2 emitted per hour of delay.

5. Electrical Grid Digital Twin (TANGEDCO) XGBoost 24h

Monitors 5 Coimbatore distribution substations (Metro Central, North IT Saravanampatti, East Industrial Peelamedu, South Residential, Kurichi SIDCO).

  • Grid Telemetry: Active power (MW), reactive power (MVAR), grid frequency (50.02 Hz), and grid capacity utilization against 1,480 MW total capacity.
  • 24-Hour Forecast Curve: Hourly load curve with shaded 95% confidence intervals and peak alerts (e.g. evening cooling surge).

6. Environmental Intelligence & Live AQI Open-Meteo Live

Direct atmospheric ingestion for Coimbatore coordinates (11.0168, 76.9558):

  • Tracked Parameters: AQI, PM2.5, PM10, NO2, CO, temperature, humidity, wind, and rain.
  • Traffic Correlation: Automatically computes the percentage share of particulate pollution generated by stop-and-go vehicular idling.

7. 'What-If' Urban Scenario Simulator Scenario Sandbox

Interactive policy sandbox for modeling disruptions before execution:

  • Traffic Scenarios: Road / Flyover Closures (10-90% restriction), Volume Surges (0.8x-2.0x), and Monsoon Downpours.
  • Energy Scenarios: Heatwave Spikes (+1°C to +8°C), EV Fleet Additions (0-20,000 EVs), and Solar Penetration.
  • Comparative Deltas: Side-by-side Baseline vs. Simulated deltas with automatic municipal recommendations.

8. Grounded AI Urban Assistant RAG Assistant

Natural language municipal assistant operating strictly on live database telemetry with zero hallucination. Answers queries on traffic, energy peaks, AQI compliance, and active alerts with concrete citations.

9. 1-Click Presentation Demo Mode Interactive Demos

Bottom control bar with 1-click presets for live demonstrations: Simulate TIDEL Park Accident, Trigger Rainstorm, Simulate Heatwave, Flyover Lane Closure, and Reset Twin.

10. Executive Municipal Scorecards & Reporting Automated Briefings

Automated daily executive briefing aggregating urban mobility health, electrical grid stability, and NAAQS air quality compliance with print/PDF export support.

3. Complete REST API Reference

Method & Endpoint Domain Description
GET /api/v1/traffic/corridors/current Traffic Citywide congestion index, average speeds, corridor summaries
GET /api/v1/traffic/predictions Traffic / ML 15, 30, 60, or 120-minute LightGBM traffic forecasts
GET /api/v1/traffic/cause/{segment_id} Explainable AI TreeSHAP 4-category causal attribution breakdown
GET /api/v1/traffic/impact/{segment_id} Downstream Impact Queue spillover, alternate routes, fuel & CO2 penalties
GET /api/v1/energy/current Energy Grid Active MW across 5 substation zones, frequency, capacity
GET /api/v1/energy/forecast Energy / ML 24-hour predictive load curve with 95% confidence intervals
GET /api/v1/environment/current Environment Live Open-Meteo AQI, PM2.5, PM10, temperature & emissions
POST /api/v1/scenarios/traffic/run Simulation Executes What-If traffic closures and surges with deltas
POST /api/v1/scenarios/energy/run Simulation Executes What-If heatwave and EV charging scenarios
POST /api/v1/assistant/chat AI Assistant Grounded natural language municipal Q&A with citations
POST /api/v1/simulation/demo-preset Demo Presets 1-click injection of TIDEL accident, rainstorm, or heatwave
GET /api/v1/reports/daily Audit & Reports Daily municipal briefing scorecard with recommendations
